About

Building quantitative intuition for biological systems.

My work combines modeling with practical machine learning, always anchored in real biological questions.

I am fascinated by the relationship between genome organisation, gene expression, and disease. Across training in physics, quantitative life sciences, and computational genomics, I have been drawn to problems where structure and dynamics interact across scales.

That interest now drives my work on how chromatin state and 3D genome architecture influence transcriptional outcomes. I care about methods that are both technically sound and biologically interpretable.
